Discover La Belle Vie

A unique adults only boutique resort.

On the quiet shore of Ban Tai, a short walk from the sand, six rooms and a lagoon pool sit inside one walled garden. Nothing here is shared with a crowd.

Every room carries original work by Anis Dargaa. No two are alike. Six keys means the team knows your name by the second morning. It also means the pool is rarely busy.

  • A hundred metres from Ban Tai beach, far enough for silence
  • Twenty five minutes from anywhere on the island
  • Private garden and terrace with every room
  • One level throughout, no step at the door

Art in the house

Anis Dargaa lives on these walls.

The elephant is the thread. It appears in red above one bed, in blue above another, as a lacquered sculpture by the pool. Each piece was chosen for the room it stands in, not bought by the metre.

Around the water, a row of terracotta warriors and a bronze gong keep watch. Guests notice them at different hours, as the light moves.

Good to know

Questions we are asked

Where is La Belle Vie located?

On Koh Phangan, in the Gulf of Thailand, less than 100 metres from Ban Tai beach. The hotel sits back from the sand, so you get the beach without the noise. Everywhere on the island is within 20 to 25 minutes. Haad Rin, famous for the Full Moon Party, is about 15 minutes away.

Is the hotel accessible for guests with reduced mobility?

Yes. The house was designed that way from the ground up. Every room, every bathroom, the common areas and the entrance are step free and wheelchair accessible, without giving up on design or comfort.

How many rooms are there?

Six, each with a private garden and terrace. They range from 40 to 68 square metres and no two are alike.

What are the check in and check out times?

Check in between 3 PM and 8 PM. Check out between 8 AM and 11 AM. The concierge is on hand from 8 AM to 10 PM for anything you need.

Do you arrange transport?

We do not run our own vehicles, but the concierge books taxis, car hire and motorbike hire. Airport transfers too.

Is there a restaurant?

No permanent restaurant. There is a self service bar with soft drinks, juices, coffee and tea included throughout your stay. Plenty of restaurants nearby deliver to the hotel. The concierge can also arrange cooking classes and themed dinners on request.

What is there to do?

The concierge arranges boat trips, island tours, nature excursions, water sports and food experiences. Tell us what you like and we build around it.

Do you welcome honeymoon couples?

Yes. We like to prepare something for the arrival. Let us know in advance.

Are there vegetarian and vegan options?

There is no kitchen on site, but many places around the hotel cook vegetarian and vegan, with delivery.

What does adults only mean here?

The hotel is for adults and welcomes guests aged 15 and over, so that the house stays calm. If you take the whole property privately, we can accommodate families with children.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation is not possible less than one month before arrival. Fees depend on the rate you booked. Non refundable rates are charged in full.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking for cars and motorbikes.

Do you provide beach towels?

Yes. We are not directly on the sand, but the pool area has sun loungers.

How can I pay?

On site, in cash or by bank transfer, to a European or a Thai account. Card payment is available for online bookings.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Free high speed Wi-Fi in every room and across the common areas.
