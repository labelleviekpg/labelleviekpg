What you get

Not a venue you share. A house you are handed the keys to.

When you take La Belle Vie privately, you take all of it. The 6 rooms, the lagoon pool, the salah, the garden, the bar. No other guests, no timetable but yours, no one at the next table.

It suits a retreat, a company offsite, a wedding party, a birthday or a shoot. The house sleeps up to 15. It is let for a minimum of 5 nights.

  • Every room on one level, no step anywhere
  • Breakfast for everyone, every morning, included
  • A self service bar with soft drinks, juice, coffee and tea
  • A concierge who knows the island and answers all day
  • Meals, transfers, a meeting room and activities on request
