Ideal for 3

Deluxe Triple Room

Teak panelling, a large Anis Dargaa elephant above the bed and a round daybed waiting in the garden outside.

The triple room has air conditioning, a tea and coffee maker, a terrace with garden views and a private bathroom with a walk in shower.

Chevron teak runs the width of the wall behind the bed, with the Anis Dargaa elephant looking down from the middle of it. A desk, a television and a mini fridge sit along the opposite side. The bathroom mixes marble and brick, with twin basins under a backlit mirror.

  • 3 guests
  • 1 King + 1 Bunk
  • 55 m²
  • 1 bathroom
