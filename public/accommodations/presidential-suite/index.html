Ideal for 2

Presidential Suite

A separate living room facing the water, a four poster bed behind white voile and a terrace with channels of water on two sides.

The suite has one living room, one separate bedroom and one bathroom with a walk in shower and bathrobes. A terrace opens onto the garden. The suite comes with air conditioning and a flat screen television.

The living room holds green velvet armchairs and a coffee table cut from a single slab of wood. A long desk sits under the window with a banker lamp on it. The bathroom is papered in dark florals and lit by a gilded mirror.
