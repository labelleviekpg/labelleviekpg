Ideal for 2

Deluxe Junior Suite

A deep blue alcove behind the bed, a blue velvet sofa under the window and a bathroom cut from black marble.

The air conditioned suite has one bedroom and one bathroom with a walk in shower and a hairdryer. A terrace opens onto the garden. The room comes with a tea and coffee maker and a flat screen television.

The headboard sits inside an arched blue panel traced with a gold rope motif. Across the room, a blue sofa and a round rattan table face the garden doors. The bathroom is lined in black marble, with a freestanding tub beside the shower.

  • 40 m²
  • 1 bathroom
