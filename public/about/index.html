Our story

What "la belle vie" actually means.

It means the good life, taken slowly. The house was built out of a liking for art, for design and for looking after people properly. Those three things are still what it runs on.

La Belle Vie sits on the quiet shore of Ban Tai. It is less a hotel than a canvas. Six rooms, each made differently, each furnished to its own plan and hung with work by Anis Dargaa. Every piece has a story behind it. Every room is a small world that holds together on its own.

See the rooms
How we work

Six keys, one team that knows them all.

With this few rooms the service can be personal without pretending. Someone is here to arrange a dinner, book a boat, find the right beach for the wind that day, or simply say good morning and mean it.

Accessibility was decided before the first wall went up, not added afterwards. Every room, every bathroom, the entrance and the common areas are step free. A guest in a wheelchair uses the same rooms as everybody else, not a special one at the back.

The heart of it

The lagoon pool.

Everything turns around the water. The pool curves between boulders and planting, with loungers on the gravel and an outdoor shower cut into a white wall. Mornings are for swimming, afternoons for the shade of the salah.

Travel is not really about the place you go to. It is about the handful of hours you remember afterwards. We try to supply a few of those.

Who is here

A small house, kept by three people.

Alex

Built the house and lives in it. He chose the art, the trees and the angle of every terrace. He gets around on wheels, which is why the place has no steps.

Fah

Concierge. She knows which boat captain turns up on time, which kitchen delivers late, where the sea is calm when the wind swings round.

David

Concierge. He answers the emails, plans the arrivals and will have the transfer waiting before you have asked for it.

Your stay

Come home to happiness

Discover the hidden corners of Koh Phangan, eat well, find the balance between comfort, style and art. Whether you are after quiet, adventure or a little of both, this is where the story starts.
