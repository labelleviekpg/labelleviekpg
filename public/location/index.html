Where we are

Ban Tai,
Koh Phangan

The island

An island of beaches, jungle and very little hurry.

Koh Phangan sits in the Gulf of Thailand, between Koh Samui and the mainland. Clear water, a green interior and a culture that has not been flattened by tourism.

Ban Tai, where the house is, is the calm south coast. Long sand, gentle water, sunsets worth stopping for. Thong Sala, the island's main town and the ferry pier, is a few minutes up the road, so you get quiet without being cut off.

Getting here: fly to Koh Samui and take the ferry, about 20 minutes of water, or come by boat from Surat Thani on the mainland. Tell us your arrival and the concierge will have a car waiting at the pier.
